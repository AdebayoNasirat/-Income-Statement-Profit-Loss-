
#  Income Statement (Profit & Loss) Dashboard

# Project Overview

This project presents an Income Statement (Profit & Loss) dashboard designed to summarize and analyze an organization’s financial performance over multiple periods. The report follows standard accounting structure and highlights revenue, costs, operating expenses, non-operating income, and taxation to arrive at Profit After Tax

The dashboard is built to support **management reporting, financial review, and decision-making**, rather than purely technical analytics.


# Project Objective

* Prepare a structured **Income Statement** using accounting best practices
* Analyze revenue, costs, and profitability across multiple years
* Perform **variance analysis** to track financial performance changes
* Present financial results in a **clear, professional, and management-friendly format**

# Accounting Structure Applied

# 1️⃣ Trading Account

* Sales
* Cost of Sales
* **Gross Profit**

# 2️⃣ Operating Account

* Selling & Distribution Expenses
* Marketing Expenses
* Administrative Expenses
* Depreciation & Amortization
* **Operating Profit**

# 3️⃣ Non-Operating Income

* Interest Income
* Gain on Sale of Assets
* Exchange Gains / Losses
* Dividend Income

# 4️⃣ Interest & Tax

* Interest Expense
* Taxation
* Profit After Tax

# Key Metrics Highlighted

* Total Revenue
* Gross Profit
* Profit After Tax


# Analysis Performed

# Variance Analysis

* Year-on-year comparison
* Absolute variance (₦)
* Percentage variance (%)
* Used to identify growth trends and cost control issues

# Revenue by Region

* Analysis of regional contribution to total revenue
* Supports geographic performance evaluation

# Monthly Revenue & Gross Profit Trends

* Identification of seasonal patterns
* Highlights months with stronger or weaker financial performance


# Tools Used

* Excel: Data cleaning, financial classification, and calculations
* Power BI:

  * Data modeling
  * DAX measures
  * Interactive dashboards and visuals

# Key Insights

* Revenue and gross profit showed consistent growth across periods
* Operating expenses increased, with marketing and administrative costs having significant impact
* Regional analysis revealed uneven revenue contribution, indicating potential growth opportunities
* Monthly trends highlighted seasonality affecting profitability
